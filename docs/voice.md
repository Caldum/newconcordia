# Concordia voice: guide for interface copy

Concordia is a strategy game in the browser: players work, train, fight for real regions, vote and govern
countries. The sample content takes place in Argentina (the sample player is Camila Ríos, an Argentine
citizen and Defense minister), but **the interface language is neutral Spanish**, not Rioplatense Spanish.
English is the second supported language (ADR 0005); section 4 covers it.

Examples in this guide are quoted in Spanish on purpose: they are the copy rules for the source language.

## 1. Neutral Spanish with *tú*

- Address the player as **tú**, never *vos* or *usted*. «Elige tu país», «Confirma tu correo», «¿Ya tienes
  cuenta?», «Eres ciudadana», «Puedes cambiarlo».
- *Tú* imperatives with an accent where needed: Regístrate, Únete, Fíjate, Elígelo, Ábrelo, Corrígela, Súmate.
- Replace regionalisms: acá → aquí, compu → computadora, colectivo → transporte, plata → dinero,
  nafta → combustible, anotarse → inscribirse, chequear → revisar, «mandar» un correo → enviar,
  lindo → bueno, intensive re- → muy. «Celular» stays.
- Keep accents and opening marks (¿ ¡).
- Proper names (people, countries, regions, banks, newspapers, companies) are not changed, even when they
  are Argentine. Numbers, amounts, percentages, dates and times are not changed either.

## 2. It must not sound AI-written (summary of the humanizer skill, applied to interface copy)

- State the fact or the action directly. No «no es X, es Y», «no solo X sino Y» or filler contrasts.
- No dramatic closing lines or runs of fragments («Gratis. Sin descargas. Sin esperas.»). If they say
  something useful, join them into one normal sentence.
- No forced triads: if there are three things because it sounds good and not because there are three, keep
  the ones that matter.
- No inflated or sales words: épico, increíble, único, sumérgete, descubre, desbloquea, experiencia, viaje,
  aventura, potencia, fundamental, clave, crucial, sin precedentes, de otro nivel, al máximo, lleva tu X a
  otro nivel.
- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) as connectors in sentences: use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
  A lone dash as the empty value of a table («—») stays; so does the minus sign in figures («−4.000»).
- No rhetorical questions to build suspense and no announcements like «Esto es lo que necesitas saber».
- No chatbot filler («¡Listo!», «¡Genial!», «No te preocupes»), no exclamation marks except for a real
  achievement.
- Prefer simple verbs: «es», «tiene», «hay» over «funciona como», «cuenta con», «se erige».
- Short, concrete sentences, with the real figure or game name when there is one.

## 3. Interface rules that stay

- Buttons start with an infinitive verb and say what happens: «Crear mi ciudadano», «Entrar a combatir»,
  «Depositar 5.000». Notices repeat the verb in the past tense with *tú*: «Compraste 10 raciones».
- Errors: what happened and how to continue, without apologizing: «Falta el dominio, por ejemplo
  camila@gmail.com».
- Capital letter only at the start and in proper names. Oro and Crédito are capitalized.
- Keep a similar length in buttons, chips and tabs so the layout does not break.
- Fixed terms: ciudadano/ciudadana, región, batalla, ronda, golpe, energía, fuerza, Oro, Crédito, empresa,
  empleo, salario, Congreso, congresista, banca, ley, vicepresidenta, presidente, ministro, tesoro, mercado,
  ración, arma, combustible.
- Game rules in force: laws are voted only by the 20 members of Congress for 24 hours; on a tie the vice
  president has 12 hours to break it and their vote decides the law on the spot; if they do not vote, the law
  falls; the president does not vote. Citizenship is immediate at sign-up; during the first 7 days damage
  counts at half and the player cannot vote in elections. There are no keyboard shortcuts.

## 4. English copy

- Plain, direct English addressing the player as *you*. Sentence case: capital letter only at the start and
  in proper names. Gold and Credit are capitalized.
- Same structure as Spanish: buttons start with a verb («Create my citizen», «Enter the battle»), notices use
  the past tense («You bought 10 rations»), errors say what happened and how to continue without apologizing.
- Section 2 applies word for word: no filler contrasts, no sales words (epic, amazing, unlock, journey,
  experience, seamless, unleash), no em dashes as connectors, no chatbot filler.
- Fixed terms: citizen, region, battle, round, hit, energy, strength, Gold, Credit, company, job, wage,
  Congress, member of Congress, seat, law, vice president, president, minister, treasury, market, ration,
  weapon, fuel. Place names keep their Spanish spelling as shown on the map («Córdoba y el Litoral»).
- Numbers follow the English locale (38,450 and 36.96); the formatting helpers do it, never hand-written.
- Keep the English text close to the Spanish length; when it is longer, check the layout at 360 px.
