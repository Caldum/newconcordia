# SectionHeader

Opens each game section (Work, Market, Banks, Fronts, Congress, Press, Resources) with a plate from the
illustrations group. It is the screen's only illustration: what follows below is data.

- A `land` panel split in two: on the left the title in `title-1` and a one- or two-sentence subtitle in
  `body-l` and `text-muted`; on the right the plate.
- The plate is cropped from the center (`preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"`) and fills its half up to the
  panel edge, without padding.
- Minimum height 220 px. When the width is not enough, the plate drops below the text.
- The subtitle explains the section's main rule, without a sales tone: «Pueden captar hasta 5 veces su
  garantía».
- Not used on Home, on Entry or on single-action screens (Battle, Vote): there the Stage or the Scoreboard leads.
