The Concordia mark: a circle divided into eight regions with irregular borders, like a political map, with
one region in red (the disputed one). `concordia-mark` has white borders for light backgrounds;
`concordia-mark-on-ink` has borders in `ink` for dark backgrounds; `concordia-app-icon` is the app icon and
favicon. The «Concordia» logotype is set in Archivo 800 at 125 % width, in `ink` or in white on `ink`. Inside
the game, the disputed region can take the player's country color.
