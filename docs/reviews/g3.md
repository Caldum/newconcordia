# G3 review

Review of `feat/g3-offices` against `docs/plans/g3-offices.md` and spec section 2.
Size: large; strategy: every path that vacates an office, then counting, then locks.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Succession | High | Every vacancy of the executive goes through `game.fill_executive` in the same transaction; promotion leaves the previous office through `game.leave_national_office`, so a promoted member of Congress also passes their seat down the list. The unique index on `offices.user_id` makes holding two offices impossible even if a path were missed. |
| Counting | High | Motions count over the seats occupied when they open, by integer cross-multiplication (2 × yes > members, 3 × yes ≥ 2 × members); an empty Congress approves nothing. |
| Data migration | Medium | Interior minister rows are deleted; citizenship review falls back to the president. |
| Locks | Medium | The citizenship lock is a trigger on new requests, plus cancellation of pending ones when an office is taken; the party lock is in `leave_party`. |
| Money | Low | Minister rewards are minted with per-month idempotency keys; the ledger balances in the test. |
| Web | Low | Components only render; the database checks presidents, members and levels. |

Accepted: no vice-presidential tie-break on motions until G4; no notifications yet.
