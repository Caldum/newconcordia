# R2 review

Review of `feat/r2-holds-and-grant` against `docs/plans/r2-holds-and-grant.md` and spec invariants 1, 8 and 10
(`requesting-code-review`, `differential-review`, `supabase-postgres-best-practices`). Size: medium; strategy:
focused on money that could be spent twice or created without authority.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holds | High (double spending) | Held money sits in its own escrow account, so the balance check refuses spending it (acceptance, pgTAP). Captures and releases lock the hold row; the idempotency key is checked again after the lock, because a concurrent call with the same key could finish while this one waited (found in review and fixed; single-session pgTAP cannot race two sessions). Over-capture, closed holds and legs with their own debit are refused. |
| Minting | High (issuance) | A trigger on postings, not the callers, refuses issuer payments without a listed reason, so no future function can mint by mistake. Existing issuer kinds (`welcome_grant`, `level_reward`) are listed; tests and e2e add `test` only inside their own transaction. |
| Supply records | Medium | Append-only; backfilled; minted minus burned equals circulation (pgTAP). |
| Grant | Medium | Once per account: an early return on the existing key keeps the rations from being added twice; earlier citizens keep their old grant. |
| API | Low (return type) | `get_my_balances` is recreated with the same grant; `list_my_movements` keeps its signature. |
| Web | Low | Held money is text under the balance, not color alone; the statement no longer breaks on company counterparties. |

Accepted: the grant's food is rations until R3 converts them to Q1 food lots.
