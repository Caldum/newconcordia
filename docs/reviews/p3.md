# P3 review

Review of `feat/p3-referral-rewards` against `docs/plans/p3-referral-rewards.md`.
Size: medium; strategy: money creation first.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issuance | High | Only a share of GOLD the referral really kept from the market or got from the system; buy-sell loops net to zero. Paid as a recorded system mint, once per amount. |
| Multi-accounting | Medium | Someone can refer their own second account and get 10% of its system pay. Accepted: the anti-abuse scan flags shared addresses, and the referral must be newer than the referrer and named within 72 hours. |
| Cost of reads | Low | The share is recomputed from the ledger per referral (two indexed lookups); a referrer sees at most 100 referrals. |

Accepted: a referral who sells GOLD after their referrer claimed can leave the referrer having claimed more than
the final share; nothing is taken back.
