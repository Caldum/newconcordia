# R1 review

Review of `feat/r1-player-core` against `docs/plans/r1-player-core.md` and spec sections 1, 6 and 11
(`requesting-code-review`, `differential-review`, `accessibility`). Size: medium; strategy: focused on the
level reward and on every caller of the changed functions.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UTC game day | High (every daily limit) | `game_day` and `game_day_start` are the only callers' source of the day; the food limit, workdays and the day change job all read them. Boundaries at 23:59:59 and 00:00 UTC and other time zones are tested (pgTAP `010`, `120`); the cron and the job slot move to 00:00 UTC together (Worker tests). |
| Level reward | High (GOLD issuance) | Paid from the GOLD issuer only inside `game.add_civil_xp`, one posting per level keyed `level_reward:<user>:<level>`, so a repeat is refused by the ledger; crossing two levels pays two, XP inside a level pays nothing, negative XP is refused (pgTAP). Not callable by clients: no `execute` grant. |
| Ranks | Medium | The step is derived and capped at 75; the acceptance thresholds 100, 400 and 562,500 are tested; XP past the cap still counts. |
| Profile function | Medium (return type) | `get_my_profile` is dropped and recreated with the same grant; only this web calls it, and the generated types and the zod schema follow it. |
| Web | Low | Dates format in UTC; health and military XP have labelled meters with value text; rank names come from one shared catalog (es, en). |

Accepted: XP has no sources yet (work, training and missions arrive in R5 and Stage 3); existing development
citizens keep their strength and are reread with the new level formula without retroactive rewards.
