# R6 review

Review of `feat/r6-market-orders` against `docs/plans/r6-market-orders.md` and spec sections 5, 8 and 12.5.
Size: medium; strategy: every money path through `game.execute_trade`, then concurrency.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money | High | One function settles every trade; its legs add up to gross + tariff, captured from the hold or posted from the buyer. A buy order's refund is `limit × q − cost`, never negative because only offers with `price × (1 + tariff) ≤ limit` match, and `round` cannot push the cost above `limit × q`. pgTAP checks the escrow equals `remaining × limit` over every order. |
| Concurrency | High | A per-book advisory lock serializes posting, buying, withdrawing and cancelling, and every row touched is also locked `for update`. Idempotency keys on orders, holds and ledger postings make retries safe. |
| Taxes | Medium | VAT and tariff to the market's treasury, fee to the sink, export tax on the seller's net to the production country's treasury in the market's currency. |
| Data migration | Low | Existing offers get `open`, `partial`, `filled` or `cancelled` from their trades and withdrawal movements. |
| Web | Low | The tab only posts and lists; the held amount is `lockedAmount`, a tested pure function. |

Accepted: no expiry until the specification gives a duration; licenses stay off (PD-03).
