# R4 review

Review of `feat/r4-automatic-food` against `docs/plans/r4-automatic-food.md` and spec section 1 and assumption
6 (`requesting-code-review`, `differential-review`, `supabase-postgres-best-practices`). Size: medium; strategy:
walk the interval replay against the recharge, tick by tick.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replay | High (energy economy) | Ticks are replayed in order with the recharge computed at each tick; the recharge progress is kept as when spending. The pgTAP cases were computed by hand tick by tick (61 after one interval, 100 after catching up four, the excess of Q2 lost). |
| Concurrency | Medium | `settle_food` locks the player's stats row; spending and adding energy settle inside the same lock, so two requests never eat the same interval twice; settling again in the same interval eats nothing. |
| Cost | Medium | The loop stops at the first full bar or when food runs out, so a month away is about ten iterations, not 8,640. |
| Reads that write | Medium | `get_my_profile` and `get_my_inventory` are now volatile; PostgREST calls them with POST through `rpc`, as the web already does. |
| Removed features | Low | Manual eating and the 200-per-day cap are gone (not in the specification); `meals` and `food_days` remain as history. |
| Web | Low | The inventory explains automatic food; the profile refetches every 5 minutes so the bar shows meals. |

Accepted: a meal that would land in the same second as a spend is applied before the spend (settle first).
