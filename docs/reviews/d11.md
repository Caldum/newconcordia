# D11 review

Review of `feat/d11-market` against `docs/plans/d11-market.md` (`requesting-code-review`,
`differential-review`, `accessibility`). Size: large; strategy: focused on the purchase transaction, on
goods held by offers and on who may sell what.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase | High (money and goods) | Three postings from the buyer (seller's share, VAT plus tariff, fee) in one transaction with the offer update and the delivery; idempotent per key. pgTAP: acceptance, tariff on imports, overdrafts, more than available, own offers. |
| Goods held by offers | High (duplication) | Posting takes units out with an update guarded by the non-negative check; withdrawing returns only what is left (pgTAP). |
| Who sells what | High (permissions) | Company stock only through `game.own_company`; deliveries only to own companies (pgTAP). |
| Prices | Low | Best price and 24-hour average weighted by units (pgTAP). |
| Web | Medium | Totals computed with the database's formulas and its rates (VAT, tariff and fee come from `market_summary`); goods buttons with `aria-pressed` and a spoken price; one idempotency key per purchase and offer. |

Findings fixed during review: taking goods out with an upsert failed the check before finding the row (now
an update); the goods buttons read «Racionesdesde 12,60» to screen readers; optional parameters moved last
so the generated types allow omitting them.

Accepted: players browse their own country's market; looking at other markets is a later improvement.
