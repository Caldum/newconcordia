| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Results | High | D'Hondt ranks every quotient with ties to the older party and takes exactly the seats in play; lists below the threshold are excluded before ranking. | Calendar | Medium | Registration now opens when the previous election takes office, for party leaders too; G1's suite still passes. |

| Web | Low | Forms only collect names; the database checks membership, levels and terms. |
