# G1 review

Review of `feat/g1-parties` against `docs/plans/g1-parties.md` and spec sections 2 and 3.
Size: large; strategy: the election state machine, then money, then privacy.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elections | High | `advance_election` locks the election and only moves forward by the clock; the snapshot is inserted once, when the vote opens, from members who joined before candidacies closed. A unique (election, voter) makes a second ballot impossible under concurrency. Found and fixed during testing: a lookup that created the election inside a `select … for update` could not see its own new row. |
| Money | Medium | Founding posts 50 GOLD to the sink (a recorded burn) in the same transaction as the party; donations are ordinary postings into the party's account. The ledger balances in every currency in the test. |
| Privacy | Medium | `game.ballots` has RLS and no grants; no public function returns who voted for whom, and totals appear only once proclaimed. |
| Membership | Medium | The citizen row lock serializes founding, joining and leaving; changing country removes the membership through a trigger, also replacing a leader. |
| Web | Low | Components only render; the phase comes from the tested `electionStep`. |

Accepted: no anti-fraud checks yet (`incident` is reserved); the clock's new cron deploys with the next clock
deploy.
