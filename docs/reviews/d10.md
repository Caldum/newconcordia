# D10 review

Review of `feat/d10-companies` against `docs/plans/d10-companies.md` (`requesting-code-review`,
`differential-review`, `accessibility`). Size: large; strategy: focused on the workday transaction (energy,
wage, tax, production) and on who may act on a company.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workday | High (money and energy) | One row per player and game day (primary key), idempotent per key; energy, wage, tax and production in one transaction, so a refused day changes nothing (pgTAP: no energy spent when the company cannot pay). |
| Company ownership | High (permissions) | Every management function locks the company through `game.own_company`, which compares with `auth.uid()`; other players get `not_your_company` (pgTAP). |
| Gold sinks | Medium | Founding and upgrades post to the Gold sink; money still sums to zero per currency (pgTAP). |
| Production | Medium | Raw at the region's yield; products limited by points and inputs, leftover points kept (pgTAP with rations). |
| Web | Medium | One idempotency key per action, kept across retries; amounts parsed to hundredths; errors mapped per code; tables with captions; tabs with `aria-current`. |

Findings fixed during review: `work` took the company first, so the generated type forced callers to send
one; it now takes the key first and the company is optional. Upgrade prices came from the web; they now come
from `list_my_companies`.

Accepted: the found-company summary shows the 50 % base yield until deposits exist (D22); selling and moving
goods wait for D11 and D12.
