# D08 review

Review of `feat/d08-profile` against `docs/plans/d08-profile.md` (`requesting-code-review`,
`differential-review`, `accessibility`). Size: medium; strategy: focused on the energy arithmetic, the new
API function and the client swap, which every screen depends on.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | High (every action spends it) | Read computes without writing; spending settles under `for update` and keeps the minutes toward the next point. pgTAP covers the acceptance test, the cap, partial progress, energy above the maximum, invalid amounts and players without a citizen. |
| API | Medium (new public function) | `get_my_profile` is `security definer`, filters on `auth.uid()`, granted to `authenticated` only. pgTAP checks another player's row never comes back and visitors cannot call it. New tables have RLS and no grants (invariants test). |
| Client swap | Medium (all screens) | `auth-js` + `postgrest-js` with supabase-js's storage key and headers, so existing sessions survive. Only `auth` and `rpc` were in use. Initial JS 167.7 → 147.2 kB with the game bar included. |
| Auth errors | Low | auth-js 2.117 delivers 5xx as retryable errors; the classification now looks at status and message. Tests use the real error shapes. |
| Accessibility | Medium | The bar's meter has a name and a spoken value; the avatar link names the player; stat cards are labeled groups; axe passes on `/profile`; the bar wraps instead of scrolling sideways at 360 px (e2e). |

Findings fixed during review: the bar overflowed 360 px once the meter and avatar were in it; the
citizenship row showed the sign-up date instead of the current citizenship's.

Accepted: medals, job and company rows of the Profile canvas wait for D10 and D27; ranks are numbers until
the war modules name them (ADR 0009).
