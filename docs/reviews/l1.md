# L1 review

Review of `feat/l1-anti-abuse` against `docs/plans/l1-anti-abuse.md` and spec sections 12.6 and 12.7.
Size: medium; strategy: the hook can break every request, so its failure modes first.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-request hook | High | Runs for every request: it returns at once for visitors and read-only transactions, writes one upsert otherwise, and only raises `rate_limited`. Removal is one statement (ADR 0037). |
| Privacy | Medium | Addresses stay in `auth.sessions`; signals and screens show account names only, to admins. |
| Signals | Low | Unique by kind and reference, so the hourly scan is idempotent. |

Accepted: no device fingerprinting; thresholds are configuration.
