# G6 review

Review of `feat/g6-central-bank` against `docs/plans/g6-central-bank.md` and spec section 4.
Size: medium; strategy: settlement and rounding first, then who acts for the Central Bank.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settlement | High | Each take is one transaction: the taker's payment and the capture from the maker's hold, under an advisory lock per pair and a row lock on the offer; repeated keys return the first result. |
| Rounding | High | The currency side rounds down on every take and the buy-GOLD lock rounds up, so captures never exceed the hold; the leftover hundredth is released on fill or withdrawal (tested). |
| Central Bank authority | High | Every bank action checks the governor's office at call time; dismissal paralyzes the bank (tested). |
| Authorizations | Medium | Row-locked, single use, company located in the country, public listing. |
| Web | Low | The screen previews locks with the same rounding; the database decides. |

Accepted: no automatic matching (consistent with ADR 0022); no FX fee until the owner sets one.
