# W2 + W3 review

Review of `feat/w2-battles` against `docs/plans/w2-battles.md` and spec sections 6, 8, 11 and 12.5.
Size: large; strategy: round boundaries and concurrency first, then the formula and its order of effects.

| Area | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Round boundaries | High | Rounds are fixed hours from the launch; an attack records the round of its own instant under a share lock, and settlement (update lock) waits for it. A late settlement sums exactly the attacks of each hour. |
| Idempotency | High | One attack per key, serialized by the player's stats row; energy, weapon use, XP and the injury happen once. |
| Formula | Medium | Numeric arithmetic in the database; the web preview uses integer arithmetic and the same test values (88). |
| Annexation | Medium | Owner change and log in the same transaction as the fifth round; one active battle per region by a partial unique index. |
| Live score | Low | Polling every 5 seconds instead of Realtime (ADR 0033). |

Accepted: alliances and travel arrive with W5; resistance battles with W4.
